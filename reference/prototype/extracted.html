Sight UNESCO World Heritage

Seongsan Ilchulbong

284-12 Ilchul-ro, Seongsan-eup, Seogwipo-si, Jeju-do

Best light · 06:30–07:20
12 photos

Why it belongs on the route

A 5,000-year-old volcanic tuff cone rising from Jeju's eastern edge. The short climb rewards an early start with a crater rim washed in amber light and broad views toward Udo Island. Allow extra time for the lower coastal path and women divers' performance area.

Time needed1.5–2 hours TrailModerate · stairs Adult entry₩5,000

Visit notes & remarks

  • Arrive by 06:20 for the lower sunrise viewpoint; summit ticketing begins at 07:00 in October.
  • The final summit section is a continuous staircase. Bring water and use the restroom near the ticket office.
  • If cloud closes in, switch to the coastal loop and move Cafe Gongbech earlier.
"Bring the small film camera. The north side of the crater catches the first light."
